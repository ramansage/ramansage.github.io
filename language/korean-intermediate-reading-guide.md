# Korean Intermediate → Advanced Grammar
### Extension of `korean-learning-guide.md`

**Prerequisite:** You already know Hangul, batchim/linking, basic particles (은/는, 이/가, 을/를, 에/에서, 도, 만…), ~아요/어요 conjugation, past/future basics, 이다/있다/없다, question words, high-frequency beginner vocabulary, and speech-level *awareness* from the beginner guide.

**This file does *not* reteach the alphabet, basic SOV, or dictionary-form survival phrases.** It **extends** those foundations into the grammar and patterns you need for **manhwa/webtoons, drama dialogue, essays, short stories, and literature**.

**How to use**
1. Keep the beginner guide open as a reference for forms you forget.
2. Work section by section; each pattern includes structure + nuance + examples (Korean + English).
3. For reading practice: mine endings first, then connectors, then vocabulary.

# 1. From Beginner Endings to Attitude Endings

You know ~아요/어요, ~습니다, ~았어/었어, ~(으)ㄹ 거예요. Intermediate Korean is mostly **what comes after the stem** to encode speaker attitude, evidence, and interpersonal stance.

## 1.1 Realization & Discovery

| Ending | Nuance | Example | English |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~네(요) | Noticing now; mild surprise | 비가 오네. | Oh, it’s raining. |
| ~군요 / ~는군요 | Realization (more reflective) | 여기였군요. | So it was here. |
| ~구나 / ~는구나 | Realization (plain/self-talk) | 너였구나. | So it was you. |
| ~다니 | Reacting to surprising news | 벌써 떠났다니… | (You mean) already left… |
| ~다니깐 / ~다니까(요) | Insisting after resistance | 모른다니까! | I *said* I don’t know! |
| ~더라고(요) | Reporting what I experienced | 생각보다 어렵더라고. | It was harder than I thought. |
| ~더라 | Vivid recall (plain) | 그때 눈이 오더라. | It was snowing then (I recall). |
| ~더니 | Observed X, then Y / change | 웃더니 울었어. | (He) smiled then cried. |
| ~더니만 | Same, often narrative | 가더니만 안 돌아와. | Left, and hasn’t come back. |

**Contrast**
- 춥**네**. — It’s cold (I just noticed).  
- 춥**구나**. — So it’s cold… (processing, often alone).  
- 춥**더라고**. — (When I was there) it was cold (telling you).  

## 1.2 Shared Knowledge, Soft Assertion, Soft Contrast

| Ending | Nuance | Example | English |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~잖아(요) | You know…; reminder; obvious | 내가 말했잖아. | I told you (remember?). |
| ~거든(요) | Explanatory “because”; soft claim | 바빴거든. | (It’s that) I was busy. |
| ~는데(요) | Background; soft but; prompt reply | 좋은데요. | It’s good though… / Well… |
| ~은/ㄴ데(요) | Same, descriptive/past | 예쁜데 비싸. | Pretty, but expensive. |
| ~는걸(요) | Mild counterinfo | 괜찮은걸. | It’s actually fine. |
| ~지(요) / ~죠 | Confirm; soft assertion | 맞지? | Right? |
| ~잖아도 | even knowing that [less common oral] | — | — |
| ~긴 해 | concede partial truth | 어렵긴 해. | It *is* hard (I’ll give you that). |
| ~기는 한데 | concede + contrast | 좋기는 한데… | It’s good, but… |

**거든 traps**
1. **Explanatory:** 안 갔어. 아프거든. — Didn’t go. (Because) I’m sick.  
2. **Conditional (if):** 힘들거든 쉬어. — If it’s hard, rest.  

## 1.3 Intention, Will, Guess

| Ending | Nuance | Example | English |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~(으)ㄹ게(요) | Speaker’s promise/commitment | 내가 할게. | I’ll do it. |
| ~(으)ㄹ래(요) | Want to; “will you?” | 갈래? | Wanna go? |
| ~(으)ㄹ까(요) | Shall we? / I wonder | 갈까? | Shall we go? / Should I go? |
| ~(으)려고 | intending to | 말하려고 했어. | I was going to say. |
| ~(으)려던 참이다 | was just about to | 나가려던 참이었어. | I was just about to leave. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 테니(까) | since (I/they) will… | 내가 할 테니까 쉬어. | I’ll do it, so rest. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 텐데 | would/must be; concern | 바쁠 텐데. | (They) must be busy… |
| ~(으)ㄹ걸(요) | guess; or “should have” regret | 갔을걸. | Probably went. / Should’ve gone. |
| ~겠- | conjecture / volition / polite offer | 춥겠다. / 제가 하죠. | Must be cold. / Let me do it. |
| ~(으)ㄹ지도 모르다 | might | 올지도 몰라. | Might come. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 수도 있다 | might / possible that | 늦을 수도 있어. | Might be late. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 | no way / can’t be | 그럴 리가 없어. | No way. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 만하다 | worth doing; decently | 볼 만해. | Worth watching. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 뻔하다 | almost did (but didn’t) | 넘어질 뻔했어. | Almost fell. |
| ~(으)ㄹ 지경이다 | on the verge of | 울 지경이야. | About to cry. |
| 거의 ~을 뻔 | nearly | 거의 죽을 뻔했어. | Nearly died. |

**(으)ㄹ게 vs (으)ㄹ 거예요**
- 내가 **갈게**. — I’ll go (I decide/promise, often for *you*).  
- 내일 **갈 거예요**. — I will go tomorrow (plan/neutral future).  

## 1.4 Hearsay, Inference, Appearance

| Pattern | Nuance | Example | English |
|---------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~나 보다 | seems (from evidence) | 화났나 봐. | Looks mad. |
| ~ㄴ/은/는 모양이다 | seems (observation) | 바쁜 모양이야. | Seems busy. |
| ~ㄴ/은/는 것 같다 | seems / I think | 비가 올 것 같아. | Looks like rain. / I think it’ll rain. |
| ~ㄴ/은/는 듯하다 | seems [slightly written] | 잠든 듯하다. | Seems asleep. |
| ~는 듯이 / ~ㄴ 듯이 | as if | 울는 듯이 웃었다. | Smiled as if crying. |
| ~다고 하다 / ~대(요) | they say | 내일 온대. | (They) say (he’s) coming tomorrow. |
| ~다고 들었다 | I heard that | 이사 간다고 들었어. | I heard (they) moved. |
| ~다지? | I heard… right? | 내일 시험이라지? | Exam tomorrow, right? (heard) |
| ~라더라고 | was told to / heard | 조심하라더라고. | (They) told me to be careful. |
| ~다더라 | I hear/heard [gossip tone] |  ent 잘나갔다더라. | Heard (they’re) doing well. |
| ~(으)ㄴ/는가 보다 | same family as 나 보다 | 없는가 보다. | Guess there isn’t any. |
| ~기 십상이다 | prone to (often bad) | 감기 걸리기 십상이야. | Easy to catch a cold. |
| ~기 마련이다 | bound to / naturally | 실수하기 마련이야. | People are bound to err. |
| ~기 일쑤이다 | often does (bad habit) | 늦기 일쑤야. | (He’s) always late. |

**것 같다 vs 나 보다**
- **것 같다**: personal judgment / soft opinion (very frequent).  
- **나 보다**: inference from what you observe/hear.

# 2. Complex Clause Connectors (Full Intermediate Set)

Beginner: ~고, ~아서/어서, ~지만, ~(으)면, ~(으)니까.  
Extend:

## 2.1 Cause / Reason Spectrum

| Pattern | Nuance | Example | English |
|---------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~아서/어서/해서 | natural cause → result; sequential “and then” | 피곤해서 잤어. | Was tired so slept. |
| ~(으)니까 | reason for command, suggestion, claim, discovery | 늦었으니까 뛰어. | It’s late, so run. |
| ~기 때문에 | explicit because | 비 때문에 취소됐어. | Cancelled because of rain. |
| ~는 바람에 | unexpected negative cause | 넘어지는 바람에 다쳤어. | Hurt because I fell. |
| ~는 탓에 | blame-oriented cause | 방심한 탓에 졌어. | Lost due to carelessness. |
| ~는 덕분에 | thanks to (positive) | 네 덕분에 살았어. | Survived thanks to you. |
| ~느라고 | while occupied doing X (excuse; subject same) | 공부하느라고 못 왔어. | Couldn’t come—was studying. |
| ~길래 | because of what I observed → I decided | 비가 오길래 우산 가져왔어. | It was raining so I brought an umbrella. |
| ~기에 | because [written/formal] | 때가 이르기에… | As the time is early… |
| ~는지라 | as/since [litt.] | 뜻이 있는지라… | As there was intent… |
| ~故로 / ~므로 | therefore [very formal/written] | 그러므로 | Therefore |
| ~느니만큼 | insofar as [written] | 중요한 일이느니만큼… | Insofar as it’s important… |
| ~고 해서 | partly because (among reasons) | 비도 오고 해서 집에 있었어. | It was raining and all, so I stayed home. |
| ~는 관계로 | due to (official) | 사정상 관계로… | Due to circumstances… |

**아서 vs 니까 (productive rule)**
- Soft personal reason / sequence → **아서/어서**  
- Commands, suggestions, logical justification, “now that I know” → **니까**  
- ✗ 피곤하**아서** 쉬어. → often odd; prefer 피곤하**니까** 쉬어.

## 2.6 Choice, Listing, Preference

| Pattern | Nuance | Example | English |
|---------|--------|---------|---------|
| ~거나 | or | 걷거나 뛰어. | Walk or run. |
| ~든지 ~든지 | whether A or B | 가든지 말든지 | whether you go or not |
| ~거나 말거나 | whether or not | 듣거나 말거나 | whether (they) listen or not |
| ~느니 | rather than (do A) | 죽느니 거지. | Rather beggar than die. (set tone) |
| ~기보다는 | rather than | 포기하기보다는 도전할게. | Rather than give up, I’ll try. |

# 3. Noun Modification & Embedding (Reading Critical)

## 3.1 Adnominal (관형형) Forms — Full Chart

| Type | Ending | Example | English |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| Action present | ~는 | 나를 바라보는 눈 | eyes that look at me |
| Action past | ~은/ㄴ | 잃어버린 기억 | lost memory |
| Action future/conjecture | ~을/ㄹ | 마주할 운명 | fate to face |
| Descriptive present | ~은/ㄴ | 예쁜 꽃 | pretty flower |
| Descriptive past | ~던 / ~았/었던 | 한때는 빛났던 꿈 | dream that once shone |
| Retrospective habitual | ~던 | 자주 가던 카페 | café I used to go to |
| Progressive past attr. | ~고 있는 / ~고 있던 | 웃고 있던 아이 | child who was smiling |
| Past experience attr. | ~은 적 있는 | 가 본 적 있는 곳 | place I’ve been to |

**던 vs 은/ㄴ (past)**
- **은/ㄴ**: completed, neutral past fact — 어제 **먹은** 빵  
- **던**: incomplete, interrupted, or habitual past — 읽**던** 책 (the book I was reading / used to read)  
- **았/었던**: clearly ended past state — 친했**던** 사이  

## 3.2 Stacked Modifiers (Literary Density)

Read **from the noun backward**.

- 차갑게 식어 버린 커피  
  — coffee that had gone completely cold  
- 더 이상 되돌릴 수 없는 말  
  — words that can no longer be taken back  
- 그가 남기고 간 마지막 편지  
  — the last letter he left behind  
- 아무도 모르는 사이에 사라져 버린 사람들  
  — people who vanished without anyone knowing  

## 3.3 Nominalizers

| Form | Role | Example | English |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| ~는 것 / 거 | fact/act of | 떠나는 것 | leaving |
| ~기 | -ing as noun (topics, emotions, ability) | 잊기가 어렵다 | hard to forget |
| ~음/ㅁ | abstract noun [written] | 그의 죽음 | his death |
| ~냐는 것 | whether… | 옳으냐는 것 | whether it is right |
| ~다는 것 / ~다는 사실 | the fact that | 배신했다는 사실 | the fact of betrayal |
| ~기 마련 / ~기 짝이 없다 | set patterns | 슬프기 짝이 없다 | extremely sad [litt.] |

**기 vs 는 것**
- **기**: short, idiomatic after 쉽다/어렵다/싫다/좋다/시작하다…  
  - 한국어 배우**기**가 재미있어.  
- **는 것**: freer clause noun  
  - 네가 떠나는 **것**은 상상하기 싫어.  

## 3.4 Quoted & Reported Structures (Complete)

| Pattern | Function | Example | English |
|---------|----------|---------|---------|
| ~다고 (말)하다 | report statement | 죽는다고 했다 | said (he) would die |
| ~냐고 하다 / 묻다 | report question | 가냐고 물었다 | asked if (I) was going |
| ~라고 하다 | report command/name | 오라고 했어 | told (me) to come |
| ~자는 제안 / ~자고 하다 | report suggestion | 같이 가자고 했어 | suggested going together |
| ~대(요) | colloquial report | 싫대 | says no |
| ~재(요) | colloquial suggestion report | 가자재 | says let’s go |
| ~래(요) | colloquial command/want report | 오래 | says to come / wants to come* |
| ~다면서(요) | I heard… isn’t that so? | 운다면서? | Heard you cried? |
| ~으라는 듯이 | as if ordering | 가라는 듯이 문을 열었다 | opened door as if saying go |
| ~고 생각할 수 없다 | can’t think that | — | — |
| ~다고 해서 | just because | 유명하다고 해서… | Just because famous… |
| ~느냐고 / ~냐며 | narrative question style | 왜 왔느냐며 물었다 | asked why (I) came |

**Compression chain (manhwa)**

| 해요체 | ~아요/어요, ~네요, ~죠 | default polite |
| 해체 | ~아/어, ~지, ~냐, ~ㄹ게 | friends, enemies, romance |

| ~았/었으면 좋겠다 | wish | 왔으면 좋겠어 | I wish (they) came |

| ~다니 믿을 수 없다 | can’t believe that | — | — |
| ~다니 슬프다/화난다 | emotion at news | 떠난다니 슬퍼 | sad that (they) leave |
| ~기 바란다 | hope [formal] | 성공하기 바란다 | hope (you) succeed |
| ~을/ㄹ 것이다 | will / conjecture [written] | 돌아오지 않을 것이다 | will not return |
| ~나 싶다 | wonder if / feel that | 끝인가 싶었다 | wondered if it was the end |
| ~는가 하면 | and on the other hand | 웃는가 하면 운다 | smiles, then cries |
| ~는 줄 알다 | thought that / know how | 온 줄 알았어 | thought (they) came |
| ~는 줄 모르다 | didn’t know that | 네가 있는 줄 몰랐어 | didn’t know you were there |
| ~을/ㄹ 줄 알다 | know how to | 할 줄 알아 | know how to do |
| ~지 싶다 | feel that [older] | 비가 올 듯지 싶다 | — |
| ~기 짝이 없다 | extremely [litt.] | 안타깝기 짝이 없다 | utterly regrettable |
| ~기 그지없다 | utterly [litt.] | 유감스럽기 그지없다 | utterly regrettable |

**줄 알다 ambiguities**
1. 네가 **간 줄 알았어**. — I thought you had gone.  
2. 운전**할 줄 알아**. — I know how to drive.

# 14. Reading Methods (Manhwa & Literature)

## 14.1 Manhwa Page Protocol

1. **Art:** who has power, distance, emotion?  
2. **Endings only:** ~잖아, ~다고, ~ㄹ게, ~냐, ~군…  
3. **Particles:** recover roles.  
4. **Lookup discipline:** only repeating words, plot hinges, titles/register (전하, 회귀…).  
5. **One-line English summary** of the page.

## 14.2 Literary Paragraph Protocol

1. Bracket each **final predicate**.  
2. Underline **adnominal stacks** before nouns.  
3. Circle **connectors** (~자, ~으며, 그러나, 결국…).  
4. Mark **tense timeline**.  
5. Paraphrase simply in Korean or English.

## 14.3 Annotated Literary Sample

**Korean**  
그는 더 이상 예전과 같은 사람이 아니었다. 전쟁이 남긴 것은 흉터만이 아니었다. 사람을 믿던 습관, 사소한 약속조차 지키려 애쓰던 마음, 그런 것들이 어느새 그에게서 빠져 나가 있었다. 남아 있는 건, 그저 다음 날을 견디기 위한 묵묵한 의지뿐이었다.

**English**  
He was no longer the same person as before. What the war left was not only scars. The habit of trusting people, a heart that tried to keep even trivial promises—those things had, before he knew it, drained out of him. What remained was only a silent will to endure the next day.

**Mine:** 더 이상 ~ 아니다; ~만이 아니다; ~던; 사소한; 애쓰다; 어느새; ~어 나가다; 묵묵한; 견디다; ~뿐이었다.

## 14.4 Annotated Dialogue Sample

**A:** 너, 지금 뭐 하는 거야.  
**B:** …말리지 마.  
**A:** 말리긴 누가 말려. 미쳤어? 그거 들키면 우리 둘 다 끝인데.  
**B:** 이미 끝났어. 처음부터 끝장이었던 거야.  
**A:** …웃기지 마. 네가 그 말을 할 자격은 없어.

**Mine:** 말리지 마; V긴 누가 V; 들키면 끝; 끝장; ~던 거야; 자격 없다.

# 17. What “Advanced” Looks Like Next

You’re entering advanced territory when you can:

3. Feel ~잖아/~거든/~는데/~더라 without translating  
4. Distinguish 피동/사동 perspective instantly  
5. Read modern short stories with sparse dictionary use
