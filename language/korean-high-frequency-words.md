# Korean High-Frequency Word List
### Core Vocabulary by Usefulness (Beginner → Lower Intermediate)

**Goal:** Give you a **drillable frequency-first word bank** that complements the [beginner guide]({{ '/language/korean-learning-guide/' | relative_url }}) and the [intermediate grammar]({{ '/language/korean-intermediate-reading-guide/' | relative_url }}). Those guides already teach *how* words work; this page is for **what to memorize first**.

**How to use**
1. Learn in **bands** (A → E), not all at once. 10–15 items/day + one original sentence each.
2. Prefer **Hangul** over romanization as soon as you can read.
3. For nouns, note a natural particle pair when you can (e.g. 집에, 책을).
4. For verbs/adjectives, learn the **dictionary form (~다)** and one polite present (가요, 좋아요).
5. Revisit band A weekly — function words carry most of every sentence.

**Notation**

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **한글** | Korean |
| *romanization* | Revised Romanization bridge only |
| (n) (v) (a) (adv) (part) | Noun / Verb / Adjective (descriptive verb) / Adverb / Particle |
| 해요체 | Polite informal (default spoken target) |

> Frequency here is **learner-priority frequency** (everyday speech + simple reading), not a pure corpus rank. Particles and copulas are listed early because they unlock everything else.
