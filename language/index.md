# Language Learning Guides

Self-study grammar and vocabulary notes for **Korean** and **Swedish**, from beginner foundations through intermediate/advanced reading grammar — plus focused word lists for daily drilling.

| Level | Guide |
|-------|--------|
| Beginner | [Korean Learning Guide]({{ '/language/korean-learning-guide/' | relative_url }}) |
| Intermediate → Advanced | [Korean Intermediate → Advanced Grammar]({{ '/language/korean-intermediate-reading-guide/' | relative_url }}) |

- **Beginner:** Hangul, particles, conjugations, core vocab, dialogues  
- **Extension:** attitude endings, complex connectors, passive/causative, honorifics, manhwa & literature reading patterns  
- **Word list:** frequency bands A–E (glue words → verbs → nouns → connectors → numbers) with a 14-day drill plan  
- **Adjectives & adverbs:** descriptive-verb families, degree scales, manner/time/stance adverbs, collocations

## How to use

1. Start with the **beginner** guide for that language.  
2. Drill the **high-frequency word list** in bands (10–15 items/day + one sentence each).  
3. Use **adjectives & adverbs** when you can form basic sentences but descriptions feel thin.  
4. Move to the **intermediate/advanced** file as an extension — it does not reteach the basics.  
5. Read with a notebook or Anki; mine example sentences, not isolated words only.
